# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers, founders, and technical leads evaluating Van for product engineering roles. They need to understand his positioning, strongest public work, practical engineering judgment, and contact path in a few minutes.

## Product Purpose

Present Van as a product-minded full-stack software engineer who turns ideas and requirements into polished web, mobile, backend, and AI-powered products. Success means a qualified employer quickly understands what he builds, sees credible evidence, and chooses to review a case study, résumé, or contact link.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and human. Confident through evidence and craftsmanship rather than exaggerated language.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a résumé converted into a website, a generic developer template, a SaaS landing page, or AI-generated portfolio UI. Avoid glowing gradients, decorative code fragments, glassmorphism, excessive Bento grids, giant pills, logo walls, meaningless badges, floating cards, unnecessary 3D, and animation without a product purpose. Do not clone Apple or Bryl Lim.

## Design Principles

- Show products before credentials.
- Make every public claim traceable.
- Use restraint to increase signal.
- Present AI as engineering leverage with review and validation.
- Curate ruthlessly instead of showing everything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. Support semantic navigation, keyboard access, visible focus, strong contrast, reduced motion, useful alternative text, 44px touch targets, screen-reader landmarks, and reflow down to 320px.
