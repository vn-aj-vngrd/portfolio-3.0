# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are recruiters, engineering managers, founders, and technical leads evaluating Van for product engineering roles. They need to understand his positioning, strongest public work, practical engineering judgment, and contact path within a few minutes.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Van as a product-minded full-stack software engineer who turns ideas and requirements into polished web, mobile, backend, and AI-enabled products. It succeeds when a qualified employer quickly understands what he builds, sees credible evidence, and chooses to review a case study, résumé, GitHub profile, or contact link.

## Positioning

Van combines TypeScript-focused full-stack delivery, product judgment, and agent-driven engineering workflows. The portfolio proves that position through public products, technical case studies, and inspectable engineering artifacts rather than unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive through a shared link, search result, résumé, GitHub profile, or job application. They may scan the homepage first, open selected case studies, verify technical depth through GitHub and the AI workflow page, review credentials in the résumé, and then contact Van. The experience must support quick scanning as well as deeper technical evaluation on desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage introduces Van, selected work, engineering approach, experience, credentials, and contact paths.
- Dedicated routes cover project case studies, résumé, GitHub activity, agentic workflow, gear, and the portfolio design system.
- Featured work is limited to public personal projects with evidence in public repositories or deployed products.
- Employer, client, private repository, and confidential Van/Lytho details remain private.
- Claims must be traceable to repository evidence, deployed behavior, or Van's confirmation. Unknown metrics, impact, ownership, and mastery remain unstated.
- Private GitHub activity may appear only as aggregates. Private repository names and source are never exposed.
- GitHub language data is described as Linguist bytes, not lines of code.
- Portfolio content remains file-backed and typed. A CMS, database, or runtime content API requires a concrete product reason.
- The site uses the existing Next.js and TypeScript foundation, favors Server Components, and limits client JavaScript to browser state, events, and platform APIs.

## Brand Commitments

The product is Portfolio 3.0 and represents Van AJ Vanguardia. Its voice is calm, precise, technical, and human. Confidence comes from evidence, direct language, and craftsmanship rather than inflated seniority or generic marketing claims. Apple product storytelling and Bryl Lim's developer-first clarity are references for principles only; the portfolio must remain Van's own work rather than a clone.

## Evidence on Hand

- Typed profile, experience, project, credential, stack, and design-system content under `src/content/`.
- Public case studies and product links for Relay, Roleway, Viya, and ACSFI.
- Public repository and contribution evidence presented through the GitHub surfaces.
- A downloadable résumé and confirmed education and certification records.
- An inspectable design-system route and `design.md` endpoint documenting the portfolio's interface language.
- No employer or client source, private repository names, fabricated testimonials, or unverified performance and impact metrics are available for publication.

## Product Principles

- Lead with shipped products and explain the problem, decisions, and implementation clearly.
- Make every published claim verifiable and protect confidential work by default.
- Help an employer move from first impression to relevant evidence without friction.
- Present AI and agents as delivery leverage governed by engineering judgment and validation.
- Curate the strongest evidence instead of cataloging every tool, repository, or credential equally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical. Preserve semantic landmarks and heading order, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, useful alternative text, screen-reader semantics, touch targets suitable for mobile, and reflow without page-level horizontal overflow down to 320px.
