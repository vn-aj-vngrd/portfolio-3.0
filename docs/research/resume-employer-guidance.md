# Research: Employer-facing full-stack developer resume

## Summary
For approximately three years of development experience plus internships, start with a readable, targeted one-page resume; use two pages only when the additional material supplies distinct, relevant evidence. Prioritize demonstrated engineering delivery, factual outcomes, and straightforward PDF formatting—not tool inventories, invented metrics, or an alleged guaranteed ATS score.

## Findings
1. **Claim: One page is a default, not an absolute rule.** **Sources:** [MIT CAPD: Resumes](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. MIT says, “Stick to one page, unless you have extensive experience or an advanced degree,” and recommends at least 10pt type and half-inch margins. **Researcher judgment:** Three years alone does not establish a need for two pages. Draft one page first; permit two if meaningful shipped work, distinct internships, and relevant products cannot fit without harming readability. Do not preserve every internship bullet merely to document completeness, or shrink typography to meet an arbitrary limit. No reviewed vendor evidence establishes automatic rejection for two pages.

2. **Claim: Bullets should show actions and factual contributions; numbers are conditional.** **Sources:** [MIT](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/), [Harvard: Creating a Strong Resume](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. MIT recommends strong action verbs, describing how work was done, factual accomplishments, and “Quantify if you can.” Harvard recommends fact-based language that both quantifies and qualifies, and identifies failure to demonstrate results as a mistake. **Researcher judgment:** Use `Built/implemented + capability + relevant method + verified outcome`. A shipped capability, resolved failure mode, successful integration, or documented workflow improvement can be a result without a percentage. Include user counts, speed gains, revenue, or time savings only when traceable and shareable. A statement of purpose is not proof of realized impact. Keep individual contribution distinct from team outcomes.

3. **Claim: Tailoring includes choosing experiences, not merely adding keywords.** **Sources:** [MIT](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/), [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. MIT recommends a master resume and selecting experiences demonstrating the position’s required skills; Harvard advises reflecting skills the employer values. **Researcher judgment:** Maintain full-stack, frontend-leaning, and backend-leaning selections as needed. Reorder truthful skills and evidence for each posting; retain official employment titles and dates. For general full-stack roles, emphasize end-to-end delivery and relevant UI/API/data/testing/deployment evidence over a long undifferentiated stack list.

4. **Claim: Personal products are legitimate evidence alongside internships.** **Sources:** [MIT](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/). **Support:** direct evidence. **Confidence:** high. MIT explicitly includes personal projects among experiences worth selecting when relevant. **Researcher judgment:** Give recent professional engineering work the most space; include one or two strong public products when they demonstrate ownership or technical depth missing from employment bullets. Compress older, repetitive internships to a title/date line or one substantive bullet before removing stronger product evidence. Retain unusually relevant internships over weaker projects. Label products as projects rather than implying employment, customers, or commercial success. Link only public work and do not expose employer/client/private-repository details.

5. **Claim: Simple document structure reduces known parsing risks; parsing is not hiring evaluation.** **Sources:** [Greenhouse: Unsuccessful resume parse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse), [Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/). **Support:** direct evidence for risks; interpretation for recommended layout. **Confidence:** high. Greenhouse describes parsing as extracting profile fields and identifies columns, tables, headers/footers, text boxes containing contact details, graphics, image uploads, and unclear sections as possible causes of partial or unsuccessful parsing. It states a 2.5MB parsing limit and explains that a failed parse can leave the resume attached for manual data entry. Harvard advises checking formatting after PDF conversion. **Researcher judgment:** Export a text-based, single-column PDF with conventional headings and contact details in the main document body; follow any employer-specific file instructions. Check selectable text and copy/paste reading order, dates, and contact details after export. Use short visible URLs with clickable links for portfolio/GitHub rather than icon-only links; test every link and keep essential evidence in the resume itself. These are practical checks, not a vendor-certified test. The reviewed documentation does not guarantee hyperlink preservation, linked-site crawling, ranking, an ATS score, or interview success.

6. **Claim: AI assistance should preserve authentic claims; prominence of AI tooling should depend on relevance.** **Sources:** [Harvard: AI for Resumes and Cover Letters](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/ai-resumes-and-cover-letters/), [MIT](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes/). **Support:** direct evidence for editing guidance; researcher judgment for skill emphasis. **Confidence:** high for editing guidance; medium for emphasis. Harvard says generative AI can help edit bullets and identify relevant keywords but should not be the primary author; candidates should verify output, explain every line, and avoid sharing personal/proprietary data. **Researcher judgment:** For ordinary full-stack roles, keep coding assistants subordinate to engineering fundamentals and delivered work. Mention genuinely used tools briefly, preferably in a concrete workflow with human review/testing. For explicitly AI-oriented roles, foreground actual integrations, evaluations, or relevant tooling evidence. Do not equate using a coding assistant with ML expertise or assert unmeasured productivity gains.

## Contradictions
No substantive contradiction found in retained sources. Apparent tension between “demonstrate results” and “quantify” is resolved by MIT’s conditional “if you can” and Harvard’s inclusion of qualitative facts. University guidance primarily addresses students and graduates; applying its space-allocation rules to a developer with three years of work is explicitly judgment, not a documented employer consensus.
