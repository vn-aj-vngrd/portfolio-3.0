## Content boundaries

- Relay creation depends on enabled Agent capabilities. The portfolio does not imply universal availability, court reservations, money transfer, or arbitrary game updates.
- Roleway Agent can propose Workspaces, tasks, notes, and Next Actions. It does not create other record types, submit applications, send employer messages, or search an exhaustive account history.
- Public Agent conversations are illustrative demonstrations, not evidence of a live model completing a mutation. New image captions identify sample data.
- No usage, revenue, hiring outcome, or performance metrics were inferred.
